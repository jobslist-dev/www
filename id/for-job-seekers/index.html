Job seeker questions, answered.

What to expect when you browse roles, apply, and take assessments on JobsList.dev.

Ask a question

Is JobsList.dev free for job seekers?

Yes. Browsing jobs, creating a profile, applying to roles, setting alerts, and viewing your own application activity are free for job seekers.

Do I apply through recruiters on JobsList.dev?

No. JobsList.dev is designed around direct applications to companies, so your application goes to the team hiring for the role.

Can I use a different résumé for a specific application?

Yes. You can use your profile résumé or attach a role-specific résumé when applying if that version better fits the opportunity.

Are JobsList.dev assessments monitored with webcam or screen recording?

No. Assessments are designed to be timed, calm, and respectful. Integrity comes from question design, randomized draws, and server-side grading rather than surveillance.
