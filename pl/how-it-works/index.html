Operating principles

Designed around trust before throughput.

The product is meant to help serious teams and candidates move faster without making the process feel extractive.

Direct by default

Candidates apply to the company hiring for the role. JobsList.dev is not built around recruiter ownership of the relationship.

Transparent before conversion

Salary ranges, work style, role context, pricing, and assessment expectations should be visible before someone commits time.

Assessment signal without surveillance

Integrity comes from tokens, randomized draws, hidden scoring data, review audit trails, and server-side grading, not webcam monitoring.

Operationally connected

Applications, resumes, assessment invites, score review, notifications, and exports live in the same hiring workflow.

No surveillance assessments

No webcam, no screen recording, no browser lockdown. Integrity comes from sampling and server-side grading.

Private attachment broker

Support images and résumé overrides use validated attachments and short-lived signed access.

Auditable decisions

Reviews, score changes, reminders, and sensitive account actions leave an operational trail.

Direct applicant flow

Candidates stay connected to the company and the role instead of a generic testing funnel.

Who is JobsList.dev for?

JobsList.dev is for technology companies hiring software and adjacent technical talent, and for builders who want transparent roles with direct applications.

Is JobsList.dev a job board or an assessment platform?

It is both, by design. The job marketplace, application workflow, resume review, assessments, notifications, and hiring analytics work together.

Do companies have to use assessments?

No. Assessments are optional and configurable per job. Teams can leave them off, send them automatically, or send them manually after reviewing applicants.

Can candidates use JobsList.dev without taking assessments?

Yes. Candidates can browse, apply, create profiles, attach resumes, and set alerts. Assessments only happen when a company invites a candidate.
