View all FAQs

Do I need an account to create developer job alerts?

Yes. Anyone can browse filtered job searches, but saving alerts requires a free job seeker account so JobsList.dev can remember your filters and notification preferences.

Can I create multiple alerts for different developer roles?

Yes. You can save separate searches for different roles, stacks, seniority levels, remote preferences, work types, locations, and salary floors.

Are alerts based on recruiter messages?

No. JobsList.dev alerts are based on matching roles published on the platform, so candidates stay focused on direct applications rather than recruiter outreach.

Can I turn job alerts off later?

Yes. You can manage saved searches from your job alerts page and update broader notification preferences from your account settings.
