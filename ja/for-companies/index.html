Talk to us

Is JobsList.dev only for software engineering roles?

JobsList.dev is focused on technology hiring: software engineering, AI, data, DevOps, security, mobile, product engineering, and adjacent technical leadership roles.

Can we use assessments without forcing every applicant to test?

Yes. Assessment delivery is configurable per job. Companies can send an assessment automatically after someone applies, or manually after reviewing the application.

Do assessment credits affect job posting credits?

No. Listing credits and assessment credits are tracked separately so teams can understand exactly what they are buying and using.

Does JobsList.dev charge placement fees?

No. JobsList.dev is designed around transparent posting and assessment pricing, not taking a percentage of a hire's compensation.
