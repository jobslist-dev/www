Integrity without surveillance

Watching people is a poor proxy for whether an answer is their own. We designed for integrity where it actually lives — in the questions and in what each candidate is asked.

What we don't do

  • —No webcam. Nothing is filmed.
  • —No screen recording and no remote control of the candidate's machine.
  • —No browser lockdown, and no penalty for switching tabs.
  • —No automatic accusations — a person decides, never a threshold.

What we do instead

  • +Every candidate draws a different set of questions from a much larger bank.
  • +Timing is held by the server, so the clock cannot be edited in the browser.
  • +Invitation links are single-use and expire.
  • +Questions are built around supplied code and concrete situations, not recall you can look up.

Where we do surface context — for instance that a candidate left the tab — it is shown to you as a plain fact alongside the result, never as an accusation, and it never acts on a candidate automatically.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a role template

    Choose the role family and see its structure — sections, difficulty mix, question count and time limit — before you commit to it.

  2. 2

    Make it yours

    Adjust sections and time, set a pass mark if you want one, and add your own questions. Then take it yourself as a candidate would.

  3. 3

    Invite candidates

    Send single-use invitation links by email. Track who was invited, who started, and who finished.

  4. 4

    Read the sections

    Compare candidates on per-section performance rather than a leaderboard, and open individual answers only when you need to.

If you're the candidate

You will be told the number of questions, the time limit, the topics, and who is asking — before the clock starts. The clock starts when you press start, not when the email arrives.

You can change any answer until you submit. If your connection drops, you resume where you were with the right time remaining. When you finish, you can keep your result on a free profile and reuse it on future applications.

Common questions

Do you use webcam or screen monitoring?

No. Nothing is filmed or recorded, we do not lock the browser, and switching tabs carries no penalty. Integrity comes from per-candidate question sampling, server-held timing, single-use invitations, and questions anchored in supplied code — not from watching people.

How long does an assessment take?

The first-party templates are 6 questions with a 20-minute limit, set at roughly twice the time the questions actually need. Time is a boundary, not a source of pressure.

Can I add my own questions?

Yes. Start from a role template and add questions your team cares about, then preview the whole thing as a candidate before anyone is invited.

What do candidates see at the end?

That is your choice per assessment: the full breakdown, the overall score only, or a completion confirmation. Correct answers and rubrics are never shown to candidates at any setting, because that is live assessment content.

Does a candidate need a JobsList account?

No. An invitation link is all they need. At the end they are offered a free profile so they can keep the result — declining changes nothing about your copy of it.

What does it cost?

Assessments are free while the product is in beta. Usage is metered from day one so you can see what you have used, and listing credits for job postings are entirely separate and unaffected.

Which roles are covered?

7 role families — backend, frontend, full-stack, mobile, DevOps, data and engineering management — each with its own question bank. Mid-level templates ship first.
