Security and trust controls we've implemented

The goal is not to bury candidates and companies under security theater. It is to make the important boundaries clear, enforce them in the product, and give teams the evidence they need to make decisions with confidence.

Reviewed companies & moderated posts

  • Company verification support for hiring accounts
  • Job posts are moderated before they reach candidates
  • Transparent, salary-forward role listings
  • Direct applications with no recruiter middle layer

Secure attachments & résumé handling

  • Résumés and images move through a brokered attachment flow
  • Private storage bucket instead of raw user-supplied URLs
  • Magic-byte file-type validation on upload
  • Short-lived signed downloads; object paths stay server-side

Two-factor authentication & account protection

  • Optional TOTP two-factor authentication
  • Hashed, single-use recovery codes
  • Stronger verification for sensitive actions
  • Server-side session and token handling

Notification preferences & account controls

  • Granular notification preferences
  • Security and account notices you cannot accidentally mute
  • Self-serve profile and account settings
  • Clear separation of candidate and company controls

30-day account deletion grace period

  • Deletion is scheduled, not immediate
  • A 30-day recovery window before data is removed
  • Signing back in during the window cancels the deletion
  • Account-lifecycle changes are audit-logged

Data export & DSR readiness

  • Self-serve data export for account holders
  • Structured export of profile and activity data
  • Export and deletion flows designed for data-subject requests
  • No answer keys, tokens, or object paths in exports

Audit logging for sensitive actions

  • Server-written audit events for sensitive actions
  • Attachment link issuance is recorded
  • MFA and account-lifecycle events are recorded
  • Admin-only review route for the audit trail

Respectful assessments

  • No webcam or screen recording
  • No keystroke logging or clipboard capture
  • Role-based, timed assessments with revisable answers
  • Browser sample checks are advisory; trusted grading stays server-side
  • Reviewable audit trail for score changes

Payment trust

  • Stripe-hosted and embedded checkout
  • Signed Stripe webhooks verify every billing event
  • JobsList.dev never handles raw card numbers
  • Listing credits and assessment credits are billed separately

Security questions

Do JobsList.dev assessments use surveillance?

No. JobsList.dev assessments do not use webcam monitoring, screen recording, keystroke recording, browser lockdown, or clipboard-content capture.

How are attachments protected?

Attachments use a managed id-based flow with private storage, file-type validation, and short-lived signed URLs. Staff and companies receive brokered links rather than raw storage paths.

Can users protect accounts with two-factor authentication?

Yes. Users can enable TOTP two-factor authentication, receive recovery codes, and use stronger verification for sensitive actions such as account deletion.

What happens when someone deletes an account?

Deletion is scheduled with a 30-day grace period. Signing back in during that window reactivates the account and cancels the pending deletion.

Are assessment answers, keys, or tokens exposed publicly?

No. Candidate and company routes use explicit safe fields. Hidden tests, answer keys, token hashes, raw attempt tokens, and storage object paths are not returned in public payloads.
