For candidates comparing developer offers.

Salary is one signal. The best decision compares compensation, scope, stability, growth, and how the hiring process feels.

Compare the whole package

Base salary matters, but remote policy, equity, bonus, healthcare, retirement, PTO, learning budget, and on-call expectations can change the real value.

Use ranges to qualify roles early

A visible salary range lets you decide whether the role is worth applying to before investing time in interviews or assessments.

Watch the level match

A Senior title at one company may look like Staff or Mid elsewhere. Compare scope, ownership, decision authority, and compensation together.

Ask about location strategy

Remote roles may use one global range, region-adjusted ranges, or country-specific bands. Clarifying that early prevents late-stage surprises.

For companies

Salary ranges are a hiring conversion feature.

Publishing compensation does not just satisfy curiosity. It helps the right candidates self-select, reduces late-stage misalignment, and makes your role easier to compare honestly.

Post a transparent role

Publish a range you can defend

A useful range reflects level, scope, location strategy, internal equity, and what your team can actually offer to close the right candidate.

Avoid the fake-wide band

A range that is technically true but too wide to guide candidates undermines trust. State the realistic target band for the role.

Separate cash from total compensation

If equity, bonus, or on-call pay matter, explain them clearly instead of hiding everything behind one vague compensation line.

Review internal equity first

Before advertising a role, make sure the published band does not reveal avoidable inequity for people already doing comparable work.

View all FAQs

Does JobsList.dev require salary ranges?

JobsList.dev strongly encourages salary ranges because transparent compensation helps candidates self-select and reduces wasted late-stage conversations.

Can candidates filter jobs by minimum salary?

Yes. The jobs page supports a minimum salary filter, and salary-focused landing pages link directly into those filtered searches.

Should companies publish base salary or total compensation?

Companies should publish the base salary range and explain important total-compensation pieces such as bonus, equity, benefits, and on-call pay separately.

How should remote salary ranges be handled?

Remote roles should state whether the range is global, region-adjusted, or country-specific, and clarify any location or time-zone constraints.
