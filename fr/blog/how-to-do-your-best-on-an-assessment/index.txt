3:T117b,<p>An assessment is a chance to show a company what you can actually do — a fairer shot than a résumé keyword filter gives you. Ours is designed to reward genuine understanding, not test-taking tricks, so &quot;doing your best&quot; is mostly about being rested, focused, and using the format the way it&#39;s meant to be used. Here&#39;s how to walk in relaxed and finish strong.</p>
<h2>First, know there are no gotchas</h2>
<p>We don&#39;t write trick questions. Our questions test judgment and understanding — reading code, diagnosing a problem, weighing trade-offs — the same reasoning you use on the job. There&#39;s no hidden catch trying to trip you up, no negative marking, and no penalty for guessing beyond simply not earning those points. So don&#39;t overthink it looking for traps that aren&#39;t there. Read the question, apply what you know, answer.</p>
<h2>Set yourself up before you start</h2>
<p>A little preparation removes most of the stress:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pick a good time and place.</strong> You have a window, so choose when you&#39;re alert and won&#39;t be interrupted, in a spot where you&#39;re comfortable.</li>
<li><strong>Use a real keyboard.</strong> For code-reading questions, a laptop or desktop beats a phone.</li>
<li><strong>Check your connection</strong>, but don&#39;t panic about it — if it drops, your work is saved and you can resume (more below).</li>
<li><strong>Have water and a clear desk.</strong> Treat it like focused work, because that&#39;s what it is.</li>
</ul>
<p>You don&#39;t need to cram. These assessments measure understanding you already have, not facts you memorized last night.</p>
<h2>Use the format to your advantage</h2>
<p>Our runner is built to help you, not to pressure you. Lean on it:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>You can move backward and change any answer</strong> until you submit. So if a question stumps you, put down your best guess, move on, and come back — nothing is locked.</li>
<li><strong>Everything autosaves</strong> the instant you answer. You never lose work.</li>
<li><strong>The timer is always visible but calm.</strong> Glance at it to pace yourself; don&#39;t let it rattle you. Aim to leave a little time at the end for the questions you flagged.</li>
<li><strong>Answer the easy ones first.</strong> Bank the points you&#39;re sure of, then spend your remaining time on the harder ones.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Pace, don&#39;t race</h2>
<p>Read each question fully before answering — most &quot;mistakes&quot; are just misreadings. Spend your time proportional to the points: don&#39;t sink ten minutes into one tricky question while five quick ones go unanswered. If you&#39;re stuck, make your best judgment and move on; you can always return. A steady, unhurried pass usually beats a frantic one.</p>
<h2>If something goes wrong</h2>
<p>Technology fails sometimes, and we planned for it. If your browser crashes, your laptop dies, or your internet drops mid-question, <strong>don&#39;t panic — just reopen your link.</strong> You&#39;ll resume exactly where you were, with your answers intact and your time handled fairly. There&#39;s no penalty for a hiccup outside your control.</p>
<h2>On using outside help</h2>
<p>We&#39;ll be honest with you, as we are with companies: we don&#39;t watch you, and we can&#39;t stop you from opening another tab. But our questions are built to reward genuine understanding, and a copied-and-pasted answer often <em>doesn&#39;t</em> reliably win on the judgment questions — while the reasoning you actually have will. Beyond that, the assessment is there to find a role that fits <em>you</em>. Getting placed somewhere on borrowed answers usually just lands you in a job you&#39;re not set up to succeed in. Your own thinking is both the honest move and the one that serves you.</p>
<h2>After you submit</h2>
<p>When you&#39;re done, you&#39;ll be able to <strong>claim a free JobsList profile to keep your score</strong> and carry it forward — handy if you want to keep applying with us. It&#39;s optional and there&#39;s no pressure. Either way, you&#39;ve given the company a real, fair look at your ability.</p>
<h2>The mindset</h2>
<p>Come rested, read carefully, bank the easy points, revisit the hard ones, and trust the format to save your work. There&#39;s nothing adversarial happening here — just a fair, calm way to show what you can do. Relax and let your actual skills speak. That&#39;s genuinely all it takes.</p>
