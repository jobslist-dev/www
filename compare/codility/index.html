Frequently asked questions

Can JobsList.dev replace a technical interview platform?

It depends on the hiring process. JobsList.dev is best when the company wants assessment results tied to the job marketplace and applicant workflow.

Does JobsList.dev expose answer keys or hidden tests?

No. Candidate and company-safe endpoints use explicit allow-lists, and hidden grading material stays server-side.
