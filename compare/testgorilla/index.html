Frequently asked questions

Is JobsList.dev a direct replacement for TestGorilla?

Not for every use case. TestGorilla is broader across non-technical hiring. JobsList.dev is sharper when the job itself, developer assessment, applicant review, and hiring analytics should live together.

Can companies add their own engineering questions?

Yes. JobsList.dev supports company-owned custom questions, bulk import, candidate-safe previews, and secure handling of answer keys and hidden tests.
