What JobsList.dev verification means.

Verification is not marketing glitter. It is a set of product controls that help candidates and companies trust the workflow.

Company pages are reviewed before going live

New company profiles enter moderation, so public pages and jobs are not treated as ordinary unreviewed user posts.

Optional KYC adds a visible trust badge

Companies can complete KYC verification. When verified, the public company page shows a badge so candidates can see the extra trust signal.

Jobs are moderated separately

A company profile and a job post are separate surfaces. Job moderation helps keep role details, compensation, and hiring context aligned with platform expectations.

Secure workflows protect both sides

Private resumes, brokered attachments, two-factor authentication, audit logs, and notification controls reduce avoidable trust and security risk.

Why it matters

Developer hiring works better when identity and context are clearer.

Candidates want to know who is hiring before investing time. Companies want relevant applicants who understand the role. JobsList.dev makes those trust signals visible without turning hiring into surveillance.

View all FAQs

Are all JobsList.dev companies KYC verified?

No. Company pages are reviewed before publication, and companies can complete optional KYC verification. A visible badge appears when a company has completed KYC.

What does the verified badge mean?

The verified badge means the company has completed the platform's company verification workflow. It is a trust signal, not an outcome guarantee for a specific job.

Are job posts reviewed too?

Yes. JobsList.dev separates company review from job moderation so public roles can be checked for platform fit and hiring-context quality.

Why does verification matter for developer hiring?

Developers are more likely to apply when they understand who is hiring, how the role works, what compensation range is offered, and whether the company has been reviewed.
