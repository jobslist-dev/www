Trust & Security

Developer hiring works better when trust is built into the workflow.

JobsList.dev is designed around direct applications, secure files, respectful assessments, account controls, and auditable operational workflows — not recruiter handoffs or surveillance theater.

Trust controls across the product

The goal is not to bury candidates and companies under security theater. It is to make the important boundaries clear, enforce them in the product, and give teams the evidence they need to make decisions with confidence.

Candidate-respecting assessments

Role-based assessments are timed and structured, but never built around webcam monitoring, screen recording, or browser lockdown.

  • No webcam or screen monitoring
  • No keystroke recording or clipboard contents
  • Answers can be revised until submit
  • Hidden tests, answer keys, and rubrics stay server-side

Secure file handling

Résumés, support screenshots, and feedback images move through a brokered attachment flow instead of raw user-supplied URLs.

  • Private storage bucket
  • Magic-byte validation
  • Short-lived signed downloads
  • Object paths never returned to the browser

Account protection

Users can enable two-factor authentication, manage notification preferences, and use a deletion flow with a recovery window.

  • TOTP two-factor authentication
  • Hashed recovery codes
  • Non-suppressible security/account notifications
  • 30-day account deletion grace period

Company trust controls

Hiring teams get verified company surfaces, direct applicant workflows, and review trails that keep assessment decisions inspectable.

  • Company verification support
  • Application-linked assessment invites
  • Review history for score changes
  • CSV exports without answer keys or tokens

Operational auditability

Sensitive actions create server-written audit records so support and admin workflows are observable without exposing secrets.

  • Attachment URL issuance audit events
  • MFA and account lifecycle audit events
  • Maintenance worker audit events
  • Admin-only audit review route

Transparent platform boundaries

JobsList is explicit about what it does and does not promise, especially around assessments, files, and code execution.

  • Browser sample checks are advisory only
  • Trusted grading remains server-side
  • Assessment billing is separate from listing credits
  • No placement fee or recruiter layer

How this feels in practice

Candidates see transparent roles, direct applications, and assessments that explain what is recorded and what is not. Companies see application context, résumés, assessment status, review history, and exports without raw secrets in the payload.

Security questions

Do JobsList.dev assessments use surveillance?

No. JobsList.dev assessments do not use webcam monitoring, screen recording, keystroke recording, browser lockdown, or clipboard-content capture.

How are attachments protected?

Attachments use a managed id-based flow with private storage, file-type validation, and short-lived signed URLs. Staff and companies receive brokered links rather than raw storage paths.

Can users protect accounts with two-factor authentication?

Yes. Users can enable TOTP two-factor authentication, receive recovery codes, and use stronger verification for sensitive actions such as account deletion.

What happens when someone deletes an account?

Deletion is scheduled with a 30-day grace period. Signing back in during that window reactivates the account and cancels the pending deletion.

Are assessment answers, keys, or tokens exposed publicly?

No. Candidate and company routes use explicit safe fields. Hidden tests, answer keys, token hashes, raw attempt tokens, and storage object paths are not returned in public payloads.
